Got Questions? We Have Answers.

RUK Ministries is a non-denominational Christian organization. We welcome pastors and spiritual leaders from all biblical-based faiths. Our teachings and programs are based on biblical principles with Jesus Christ at the core.

After the event, you will have the opportunity to join the RUK Ministries community, a brotherhood of Christian leaders committed to supporting each other in their journey of faith and leadership. We offer ongoing training, seminars, and webinars to support your continued growth.

Attendees will walk away with a clear vision and roadmap for success in their pastoral duties, personal life, and spiritual growth. You will learn new strategies around leadership, finance, and personal development. The event aims to reignite your passion for your calling and equip you with the tools to live a balanced and fulfilling life.

The cost of the event covers lunch and dinner for all three days, as well as additional gear that we provide at the event. Please note that accommodation is not included, but we have negotiated a discounted rate at a nearby hotel.

The RUK Ministries event is a 3-day intensive program. Check-in begins on Tuesday at 9am and the event concludes on Thursday around 9/10pm.
