E03Put the responsibilities on paperDay 3+
Subject
Put the responsibilities on paper
Preview text
A clearer way to think about time away, team coverage, and the work that keeps coming to you.

Hi [First Name],

Three days away looks impossible when every responsibility stays in your head.

Put them on paper... then look at each one separately.

For each responsibility, write:

  1. What actually needs to happen? Be specific about the task and the deadline.
  2. Who can own it? Name a person, rather than writing “the team.”
  3. What would they need from you first? Information, a clear decision, or permission to act?

You may find work that needs preparation. You may find something only you can do... and something you've kept doing because nobody else has been given ownership.

That gives you a practical starting point for the conversation with your staff, family, or elders.

Revival is a three-day experience. Before making arrangements, talk with our team about the schedule and participation requirements, then work through the coverage your ministry needs.

“I can't leave” deserves a closer look... with the actual responsibilities in front of you.

Program details

Application & booking

Campaign scope
