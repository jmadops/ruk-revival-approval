## P01 · Who pastors the pastor?

Everyone knows where to find the pastor when something goes wrong.

A marriage needs help. A staff member needs an answer. Another family needs him on the phone.

But who hears what he is carrying?

The ministry can keep moving while his own faith, family, health, and finances get pushed further down the list. By Sunday, he is back in the pulpit with another message ready.

Revival gives the man behind that message three days to do the work he keeps postponing.

Christ-centered training. Experiential work. Brotherhood with male pastors and ministry leaders. Faith, Family, Fitness, and Finances.

## P02 · What comes home?

The church gets his strongest hours. His family gets the man who is left at the end of them.

He is at dinner, but his mind is still in the meeting. His wife is talking, but another call is coming in. The kids want his attention, and he is trying to find enough energy to give it.

That cost deserves a place in the conversation.

Revival is a three-day, Christ-centered intensive for male pastors and ministry leaders. It brings the man at home into the work, alongside his faith, health, and personal financial stewardship.

Come prepared to look honestly at the life behind the calling, with training, experiential work, and men who understand ministry responsibility.
