BEFORE YOU APPLY

Get clear.
Then decide.

Who is Revival for?+

Revival is for male pastors and ministry leaders. Senior, lead, executive, and men’s ministry pastors are the primary audience. If you serve in another role, describe your responsibilities in the application so the team can discuss fit.

What happens during the three days?+

The experience combines Christ-centered training, experiential work, and brotherhood across Faith, Family, Fitness, and Finances. Ask the team for the current schedule and participation requirements before making travel arrangements.

What is included in the experience?+

Revival includes the three-day program, lunch and dinner during the event, materials, and RUK gear. The team can help you plan the practical details of attendance.

Can my church support my attendance?+

You can discuss Revival with your board or elders as pastoral-health and leadership development. The RUK Ministries team can provide the information your church needs to consider attendance.

What is the Christian foundation?+

RUK Ministries is a non-denominational Christian organization with Jesus Christ at the center of its work. The team can discuss its statement of faith and any theological questions before you decide to attend.

Can I ask about privacy before sharing personal details?+

Yes. You can ask the RUK Ministries team about its privacy and confidentiality arrangements before discussing sensitive personal matters.

What happens after I apply?+

Your application goes to RUK Ministries through its existing application system. The team handles the next steps toward a coach conversation.

Where and when is the next event?+

The team can confirm current dates, location, availability, and arrival and departure expectations during the application process.
